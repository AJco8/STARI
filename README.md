## Overview
The STARI mission is to demonstrate the feasibility of interferometry in orbit. Starlight is reflected from one free-flying spacecraft to a fiber optic wire to its counterpart 10 to 100 meters away. The satellites fly in formation to keep the craft together, with one follower orbiting the leader.
