# Telecommunications
## Analysis Overview
Telecommunications is responsible for the radio communication between the satellite and the receiver on the surface of earth. The ground station will receive payload and engineering data must be sent to the ground for analysis and commands must be able to reach the satellite to adjust operations to accomplish the mission. Communication is only possible during the communication window, where the satellite is in range of the ground station and not blocked by the planet. This window must be calculated based on the range of the transmitter and receiver of the satellite and the ground, the height of orbit, and the speed of the craft. Once the window is calculated, the frequency the orbit will intersect with the window will be determined by a simulation in STK. 
The speed of the satellite in orbit relative to the station on the ground will result in a varying distortion in the frequency of the radio signal due to the Doppler Effect. The speed relative to the ground station can be calculated over the duration of the path within the communication window and the resulting distortion can be predicted and compensated for based on an analytical simulation. The communication window will likely be brief, measured in minutes. This demands a large amount of data to be communicated in a short period of time, requiring the Telecommunication system to prioritize certain types of data depending on its necessity for the mission. The data budget is calculated to find the average amount of data that can be transmitted to the ground.

## Inter-Satellite Communication
The satellites will communicate using the onboard radio system at a frequency of 440 MHz. The higher frequency allows for data to be communicated faster at the cost of a reduced penetration and range that is not needed for communication between the satellites flying together. The follower satellite will need to send data such as its position and state to the commander satellite and the commander will need to send commands to the follower.
